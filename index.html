Using Quizmix

Quizmix is built around usability with a distraction-free interface. Study without any ads, locked features or unnecessary clutter. Quizmix is designed to be simple, fast and easy to use.

Lists

All of your UST CICS courses are listed and searchable throughout the interface.

Quiz Items

Rating

After finishing the item, you can tell Quizmix how hard it was, and it will repeat the same question for you until you get it right.
